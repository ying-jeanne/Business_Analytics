Cost-Sensitive Threshold Optimization

Standard metrics like Accuracy or F1-Score treat all errors equally. In credit risk, defaulting on a loan (False Negative) costs ~5x more than the opportunity cost of rejecting a good customer (False Positive).

We designed a custom business cost function:
Cost = (FN * 5) - (TN * 1)

Optimization Results

  • F1-Optimal Threshold:
    0.34 (Approves 74.5% of applicants)
  • Cost-Optimal Threshold:
    0.13 (Approves 40.2% of applicants)

Moving to the cost-optimal threshold reduced business risk exposure significantly.

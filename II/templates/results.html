Cost-Sensitive Threshold Optimization

The U-shaped curve shows the minimal cost at threshold 0.13.

Standard metrics like Accuracy or F1-Score treat all errors equally. In credit risk, defaulting on a loan (False Negative) costs ~5x more than the opportunity cost of rejecting a good customer (False Positive).

We designed a custom business cost function:
Cost = (FN * 5) - (TN * 1)

Optimization Results

F1-Optimal Threshold: 0.34 (Approves 74.5% of applicants). Cost-Optimal Threshold: 0.13 (Approves 40.2% of applicants)

Moving to the cost-optimal threshold reduced business risk exposure significantly.

How to interpret Blackbox Models

While LightGBM provides superior predictive performance (AUC 0.764), it is a complex "Blackbox" model. In finance, we cannot rely on opaque algorithms. We employ SHAP values to bridge the gap between performance and transparency, satisfying regulatory requirements (e.g., GDPR Article 22, Fair Lending).

Global Feature Importance

We analyzed which features drive default risk globally across all customers. The results confirm that past payment behavior is the strongest predictor, far outweighing demographics.

  • MAX_DELINQUENCY: The single most important factor.
  • PAY_1: Recent payment status is critical.
  • Demographics: (Age, Sex, Marriage) have very low impact, ensuring fairness.

Strategic Business Recommendations

Automate Small Loans: Implement fully automated decisioning for loans ≤ $200k. Auto-approve low-risk applicants (Score < 0.13) to scale operations efficiently.

Profit-Centric Metrics: Shift KPIs from generic accuracy to Net Cash Flow. Align model evaluation with the 5:1 cost asymmetry between defaults and profits.

Risk Monitoring: Track "Automation Rate" and set circuit breakers if default rates spike. Retrain quarterly to adapt to changing economic conditions.

Project Overview

The Pain Point

Credit default is a real challenge for financial institutions. For small loans, the operational complexity and cost of manual review often outweigh the generated revenue.

The Balance

Institutions need to balance efficiency with compliance. Pure automation saves costs, but strict regulations require decisions to be fully explainable.

Our Intent

LendRight intends to provide an automated yet explainable ML solution for credit application review, solving the cost vs. compliance trade-off.

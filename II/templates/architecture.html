System Architecture & Explainability

Opening the Blackbox with SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations).

Why SHAP?

While LightGBM provides superior predictive performance (AUC 0.764), it is a complex "Blackbox" model. In finance, we cannot rely on opaque algorithms. We employ SHAP values to bridge the gap between performance and transparency, satisfying regulatory requirements (e.g., GDPR Article 22, Fair Lending).

Global Feature Importance

We analyzed which features drive default risk globally across all customers. The results confirm that past payment behavior is the strongest predictor, far outweighing demographics.

  • MAX_DELINQUENCY: The single most important factor.
  • PAY_1: Recent payment status is critical.
  • Demographics: (Age, Sex, Marriage) have very low impact, ensuring fairness.

Feature Impact Direction (Beeswarm Plot)

Red dots = High feature values. Blue dots = Low feature values. Right = Higher Risk.

Local Interpretability (Case Studies)

SHAP allows us to explain individual predictions. We can tell a specific customer exactly why they were approved or rejected.
